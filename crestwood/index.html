The Fund

Raised in Canada. Invested in Canada.

The Crestwood Investment Fund is a professionally managed, Canadian-domiciled vehicle built around a single idea: some of the best real estate opportunities in the country are in our own backyard, and Canadians should be the ones to own them.

The fund will finance the expansion of Everwild beyond the four locations it operates in today, into new locations in British Columbia and Ontario — from the Pacific coast and Whistler to the Ontario countryside and downtown Toronto.

Every dollar is raised from Canadian eligible and accredited investors, invested in Canadian communities, and managed by Basecamp Group, the Canadian team behind Everwild.
