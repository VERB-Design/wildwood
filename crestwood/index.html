The Fund

Raised in Canada. Invested in Canada.

The Crestwood Investment Fund is a professionally managed, Canada-focused fund built on the belief behind everything Basecamp Group does: experience is the asset. The places people return to, season after season, are the ones that last — and some of the best of them are in our own backyard. Canadians should be the ones to own them.

The fund will finance the expansion of Everwild beyond its first three locations — Fernie, Banff and Harmony, now under construction — into new locations in British Columbia and Ontario: Sooke Point on Vancouver Island, Brew Creek near Whistler, Lake Simcoe and downtown Toronto.

Every dollar is raised from Canadian eligible and accredited investors, invested in Canadian communities, and managed by Basecamp Group, the Canadian team behind Everwild.

Our Story

From the Mountains to Everwild

Basecamp Group started in the Canadian Rockies with a simple idea: build places people want to come back to. For more than 15 years, the team has developed, built, and operated hotels, resorts, and residences across Western Canada.

Everwild grew out of that work. It brings the Nordic spa tradition of hot, cold, and rest to Canadian landscapes, in places designed to slow people down. Its first three locations, in Fernie, Banff and Harmony, are under construction and open from late 2027.

Crestwood is the next step. It lets Canadians own a share of the brand’s growth, as Everwild opens in British Columbia and Ontario.
